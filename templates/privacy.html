Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you create an account, and the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, like your browser type and general usage activity, to help us keep the Service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and maintain the Service — for example, to display your expense history, generate summaries and category breakdowns, and keep your account secure. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying information to understand how the Service is used and to improve it over time. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database for as long as your account remains active. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. If you delete your account, your data will be removed within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it for legal or record-keeping purposes.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting or email delivery, who process data only as needed to provide those services and under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website. We're happy to help.
